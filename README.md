## E-commerce Sales Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive Excel dashboard built on a year of online fashion-store sales (Vrinda Store, 2022) to understand how sales move through the year, who the customers are, where orders come from, and how many orders succeed.

## Business Questions

1. How do sales and order volumes trend across the year?
Total sales were about ₹21.2M across 31,047 orders. Sales and orders peak in March (about ₹1.93M, 2,819 orders) and then drift down, with November the lowest (about ₹1.62M, 2,383 orders), roughly 16% below the March peak. The two lines move together, so the drop comes from fewer orders rather than lower order values.

2. Which gender generates more sales?
Women generate about 64% of sales (₹13.6M) versus 36% for men (₹7.6M), and place about 69% of all orders.

3. How many orders are successfully delivered?
About 92% of orders are delivered (28,641). The remaining 8% are lost: 1,045 returned (3.4%), 844 cancelled (2.7%) and 517 refunded (1.7%).

4. Which states generate the most sales?
Maharashtra leads with about ₹2.98M (14%), followed by Karnataka (₹2.65M), Uttar Pradesh (₹2.10M), Telangana (₹1.71M) and Tamil Nadu (₹1.68M). These five states together account for about 53% of total sales.

5. Which age group and gender buy the most?
Adult women are the biggest segment at about 35% of orders, followed by teenage women (21%) and adult men (15%). Adults make up about half of all orders, teenagers about 30% and seniors about 20%. Women outnumber men in every age group.

6. Which sales channels bring in the most orders?
Amazon is the leading channel with about 35% of orders, then Myntra (23%) and Flipkart (22%). These three make up about 80% of orders; Ajio, Snitch, Meesho and others share the remaining 20%.
