# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive Excel dashboard built on a year of online fashion-store sales to understand how sales move through the year, who the customers are, where orders come from and how many orders succeed.

## 🎯 Project Overview
The goal was to turn raw order data into a dashboard that answers six business questions and ends with clear recommendations.

**Dataset:** 31,000+ order records, with fields such as order ID, date, status, channel, category, amount, ship state, gender and age group.

---

## 🤔 Business Questions

### ❓ 1. How do sales and order volumes trend across the year?
Total sales were about ₹21.2M across 31,000+ order records. Sales and orders peak in March (about ₹1.93M, 2,819 orders) and then drift down, with November the lowest (about ₹1.62M, 2,383 orders), roughly 16% below the March peak.

### ❓ 2. Which gender generates more sales?
Women generate about 64% of sales (₹13.6M) versus 36% for men (₹7.6M), and place about 69% of all orders.

### ❓ 3. How many orders are successfully delivered?
About 92% of orders are delivered (28,641). The remaining 8% are lost: 1,045 returned (3.4%), 844 cancelled (2.7%) and 517 refunded (1.7%).

### ❓ 4. Which states generate the most sales?
Maharashtra leads with about ₹2.98M (14%), followed by Karnataka (₹2.65M), Uttar Pradesh (₹2.10M), Telangana (₹1.71M) and Tamil Nadu (₹1.68M). These five states together account for about 53% of total sales.

### ❓ 5. Which age group and gender buy the most?
Adult women are the biggest segment at about 35% of orders, followed by teenage women (21%) and adult men (15%). Adults make up about half of all orders, teenagers about 30% and seniors about 20%. Women outnumber men in every age group.

### ❓ 6. Which sales channels bring in the most orders?
Amazon is the leading channel with about 35% of orders, then Myntra (23%) and Flipkart (22%). These three make up about 80% of orders; Ajio, Snitch, Meesho and others share the remaining 20%.

---

## 📈 Headline Numbers
- Total sales: about ₹21.2M from 31,047 order records (average order value about ₹682)
- Women drive 64% of sales and 69% of orders
- 92% of orders are delivered; 8% are returned, cancelled or refunded
- Sales peak in March (about ₹1.93M) and fall to a low in November (about ₹1.62M)
- Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh lead on sales; the top 5 states make up 53% of the total
- Amazon, Myntra and Flipkart bring in about 80% of orders

---

## 💡 Recommendations
1. Run targeted campaigns for women customers, especially adult women, the largest segment.
2. Focus ads and inventory on Amazon, Myntra and Flipkart.
3. Keep delivery and marketing strong in the top 5 states, and look for growth in the remaining states.
4. Plan offers to reverse the decline in sales and orders after March.
